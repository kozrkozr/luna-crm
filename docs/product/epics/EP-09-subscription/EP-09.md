# EP-09 — Subscription

- **Subproject:** 001-luna-crm
- **Status:** draft
- **Covers requirements:** none yet — **proposed `R-29`** (`R-25`–`R-28` are already proposed by
  `EP-06`, `EP-02`, `EP-07` and `EP-08`). Amending the PRD is the owner's call.
- **Added:** 2026-10-08 — `ADR-023`

## Goal
Luna pays for itself: everyone who uses the app tries it free for 14 days and then pays $4.99 a
month — and anyone who does not pay can still see everything they have, while their links keep
working for crew and clients.

## Rationale
The TestFlight beta is free; the owner wants the public App Store release paid (chat,
2026-10-07). `prd.md` set a price but no way to pay, no trial and no behaviour once someone stops
paying. Without this epic the public release is free by default.

## Stories
| ID | Story | Status |
|---|---|---|
| US-051 | Start a free trial on the paywall after registration | draft |
| US-052 | View mode without an active subscription | draft |
| US-053 | See and manage the subscription in the profile | draft |
| US-054 | Be reminded before the free trial ends | draft |
| US-055 | Beta testers keep full access for 3 months after the launch | draft |
| US-056 | The terms and the privacy policy cover the subscription | draft |

## Order
1. **Spike S-7** — one sandbox purchase of the real product on a device, with the trial, through
   the chosen provider, and the server recording the account's access. It can invalidate the
   provider choice (RevenueCat, `ADR-023`) and is cheap only before anything sits on it.
2. `US-052` — the server's access record and the database rules; everything else reads them.
3. `US-051` → `US-053` → `US-054` → `US-055`.
4. `US-056` — before the public release, not before the code.

## Out of scope
- **A free tier (freemium)** — deferred (`ADR-023`).
- **A yearly plan, regional prices** — one monthly price for now (owner, 2026-10-08).
- **The web app and file storage** — both belong to the planned PRO tier (`ADR-023` decision 7,
  owner 2026-10-09); this subscription is the iOS app.
- **A yearly plan and the PRO tier** — later, in the same subscription group (`ADR-023` decision 9).
- **Charging link readers** — they have no account (`ADR-013`).

## Dependencies
`ADR-023`. Owner's App Store Connect work: Paid Apps Agreement, banking, tax, the subscription
product with a 14-day introductory offer, the Small Business Program. A RevenueCat project.

## Acceptance
All six stories accepted, and a sandbox subscription bought, renewed and lapsed on a device moves
one account through trial → active → view mode with its links still opening.
