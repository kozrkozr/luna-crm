- **Subproject:** 001-luna-crm
- **Status:** draft
- **Covers requirements:** none yet — **proposed `R-27`** (`R-25` and `R-26` are already proposed by `EP-06` and `EP-02`). `prd.md` lists file hosting as a
  non-goal; amending it is the owner's call, and this epic should not be gated until it is.
- **Added:** 2026-10-03, from `decisions/ADR-020-*.md`

## Goal
The shoot creator uploads raw files and finished photos to the shoot itself, and the client
gets them from the same link that already shows their shoot — instead of a separate
file-sharing service.

## Rationale
`ADR-005` deferred file delivery and `ADR-008` filled the gap with a pasted link. `ADR-020`
undefers it: files live on Backblaze B2, are deleted 30 days after the first upload to the shoot,
and count against a 200 GB quota per account. This epic is the creator's side — upload, early
deletion, the scheduled deletion and its warning. The client's side is `US-024` and `US-025`
in `EP-04`.

## Out of scope
- **Uploading from the iOS app** — deferred (`ADR-021`). Files upload from the photographer's app
  in a desktop browser (`US-040`).
- **Crew seeing the files** — client only for now; others later (owner, chat 2026-10-03).
- **Payment.** The 200 GB quota applies to every account with no payment until a price is
  decided (`ADR-020`, decision 6).
- **A per-file size limit** — deliberately none (`ADR-020`, decision 5).

## Dependencies
EP-02 — files attach to an existing shoot. EP-04 — the client sees them through the link
gateway (`ADR-013`).

## Acceptance
All four stories accepted, and `US-024`/`US-025` accepted in their 2026-10-03 form.
