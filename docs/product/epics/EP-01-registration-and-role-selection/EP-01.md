# EP-01 — Registration and role selection

- **Subproject:** 001-luna-crm
- **Status:** draft
- **Covers requirements:** R-01, R-13, R-14, R-15

## Goal
Anyone can register, be identified by their professional role, come back and log in later, see
their own profile, and log out — so the app works for whichever role actually creates a shoot,
not just a fixed "manager" account type (`decisions/ADR-001-*.md`, `ADR-002-*.md`), and has a
complete basic account lifecycle, not just a way in.

## Rationale
`prd.md` R-01 exists because v1 deliberately has no separate "manager" account tier — any
registered user, whatever role they pick, can become a shoot creator. R-13 (login) was added
when this epic's backlog was split into stories: registering is pointless without a way to
return. R-14 (profile) and R-15 (logout) were added from Ilona's review of the phase-03
prototype (`03-design/reviews/r01-2026-08-23/notes.md`) — the same principle applies: viewing
your own account and leaving it are separate, genuine capabilities, not details of registering
or logging in. Without this epic, no other epic has a user to belong to.

## Out of scope
- A searchable directory of registered users by role — that is the deferred crew directory
  (`decisions/ADR-003-*.md`), not part of registration itself.
- Password reset, social login, or other auth mechanics beyond basic login — not specified
  anywhere in `prd.md`; left for 04-tech, not invented here.
- Editing your own profile (changing name, contact, or role after registration) — the review
  asked for a profile *page*, not an edit flow; not invented here, see
  `02-product/open-questions.md` if this needs confirming later.

## Dependencies
None — this is the first epic; every other epic assumes a registered user exists.

## Acceptance
All four stories accepted, and a registered user's role is visible wherever their name appears
in a shoot (crew list, client view), whether they just registered, logged back in, or are
viewing their own profile.
