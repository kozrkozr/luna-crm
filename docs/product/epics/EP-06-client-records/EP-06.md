# EP-06 — Client records

- **Subproject:** 001-luna-crm
- **Status:** draft
- **Covers requirements:** none yet — **proposed `R-25`**. `prd.md` predates this capability;
  amending it is the owner's call, and this epic should not be gated until it is.
- **Added:** 2026-08-28, from the design system adopted in `decisions/ADR-017-*.md`

## Goal
The photographer keeps one record per client that outlives a single shoot — contacts, notes, and
the history of shoots they have booked — so that the second booking starts from what she already
knows about them instead of from an empty form.

## Rationale
Until now a client was two text columns on the shoot (`data-model.md`), which meant booking the
same person three times produced three unrelated strings. Two of the six mockups behind
`ADR-017` describe otherwise: a profile screen with shoot history, and notes explicitly labelled
«зберігається між зйомками» [kept between shoots].

That label is the whole point of this epic. Notes that persist across shoots — «любить м'яке
денне світло», «не любить чекати» — are the one thing here a spreadsheet genuinely cannot do,
and they are what makes the record worth keeping. `decisions/ADR-018-*.md` records why this
becomes an entity and why that does not reopen `ADR-003`.

**This gives the client nothing.** A profile is a record the photographer keeps *about* someone,
not an account someone holds. `US-010` stands: no client ever registers, and nothing in this
epic issues a credential.

## Out of scope
- **Merging two client profiles.** The dedup rule in `US-029` permits duplicates by design
  (`ADR-018`), so merging is a foreseeable want — not requested, not built (`ADR-018` open
  question 2).
- **Deleting a client.** No mockup has the action (`ADR-018` open question 1).
- **Anything client-facing.** The client's own link view is `EP-04` and is untouched: neither
  the profile nor its notes enter any link payload (`ADR-018`, Visibility).
- **A shared contact table across crew and clients.** Rejected as Option C in `ADR-018`; crew
  stay per-shoot rows.

## Dependencies
- `EP-01` — the creator must be a registered user; a client belongs to one creator under RLS.
- `EP-02` — `US-029` changes the creation form `US-002` owns.

## Acceptance
Both stories accepted, and the client shown on a shoot is the same row the profile screen reads
— one client record, not a profile screen sitting beside the old text columns.
