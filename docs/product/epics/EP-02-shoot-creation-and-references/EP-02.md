# EP-02 — Shoot creation and references

- **Subproject:** 001-luna-crm
- **Status:** draft
- **Covers requirements:** R-02, R-03, R-09, R-16, R-17, R-18, R-19; `US-030`–`US-034` cover
  none yet — **proposed `R-26`**, see Rationale

## Goal
A shoot creator has one record per shoot — client info, references, date, location, and status
— that she can create, edit, delete, and browse (list or calendar), instead of hunting across
chats and notes for what she already discussed weeks earlier (`01-discovery/personas.md`, Solo
Shoot Owner pains).

## Rationale
This is the core "one record instead of five scattered tools" premise of the whole product
(`01-discovery/vision.md`, Differentiation). Without it, there is nothing for crew or clients
to view later — EP-03 and EP-04 both depend on a shoot existing first. `US-018`–`US-021` were
added from Ilona's review of the phase-03 prototype
(`03-design/reviews/r01-2026-08-23/notes.md`): a shoot record isn't "one place instead of
scattered tools" if it can't be corrected, closed out, or browsed the way she actually works.

`decisions/ADR-017-*.md`. They are the parts of that system the current schema cannot express:
hours rather than a bare date, grouped references, and a location richer than one address plus
one attachment. `US-034` was held for its ambiguous mockups until 2026-10-17, when the owner

«Статистика».
`US-063` followed: the same list behind «Проведено», «Заплановано» and a new «В обробці».

`US-068` was added on 2026-10-18 from the owner and `Materials-Refs.dc.html`: photos and links

`US-069` followed the same day: the «Матеріали» photo preview swipes, and marks or removes a photo.

## Out of scope
- Actual file hosting (raw files, finished photos) attached to a shoot — explicitly deferred
  (`decisions/ADR-005-*.md`, amended by `ADR-008-*.md` for the Client-facing placeholder/link
  only — see `EP-04.md`).
- Undoing a delete (trash/restore) — not requested; a delete is treated as final in v1.
- Any automatic behavior tied to the "Finished" status (e.g. locking further edits) — not
  specified; the status is just a field the creator can change, nothing more, in v1.

## Dependencies
EP-01 — a shoot creator must be a registered user first.

## Acceptance
All fifteen stories accepted, and a shoot created in US-002 is the same shoot that US-003's
references, US-004's list/calendar, US-018's edits, US-019's deletion, and US-020's status all
operate on — no separate, disconnected data models.
