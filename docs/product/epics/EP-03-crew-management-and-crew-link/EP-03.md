# EP-03 — Crew management and the crew-facing link

- **Subproject:** 001-luna-crm
- **Status:** draft
- **Covers requirements:** R-04, R-05, R-06, R-07, R-08, R-20, R-21

## Goal
A crew member has one consistent place to find what they need for a shoot, see who else is
involved, and confirm or decline it; the shoot creator can manage who's on the crew, including
removing someone — instead of re-explaining logistics to each person separately and fielding
questions mid-shoot (`01-discovery/personas.md`, both personas' pains).

## Rationale
This is the crew/call-sheet half of the product that `decisions/ADR-005-*.md` chose to ship
first. Manual entry with no account required is the deliberate cold-start fallback
(`decisions/ADR-003-*.md`); the link is how an unregistered crew member reaches any of it.
`US-022` and `US-023` were added from Ilona's review of the phase-03 prototype
(`03-design/reviews/r01-2026-08-23/notes.md`) — crew management wasn't complete without a way
to remove someone, and crew members wanted to see each other's details, not just names.

## Out of scope
- A searchable crew directory with visible availability — deferred, this epic only covers
  manual entry and self-registration, not discovery of new crew (`decisions/ADR-003-*.md`).
- Changing a confirm/decline response after it's submitted — explicitly not supported in v1
  (owner's answer in chat, 2026-08-22; see `US-008-*.md` in this folder).
- Time-based expiration of a crew link — resolved as not needed: only explicit removal
  invalidates a link (see `US-006-*.md` in this folder).
- The Client viewing crew details **this way** — the Client gets a related but narrower view
  instead (`EP-04.md` `US-026`: same idea, minus notes), not `US-023`'s screen. The two are
  deliberately different, not one shared with the client added on (round r02 review,
  2026-08-23).

## Dependencies
EP-02 — a crew member is always added to an existing shoot, never created standalone.

## Acceptance
All seven stories accepted, and a crew member added in US-005 is the same person whose link is
generated in US-006, viewed in US-007, used to confirm/decline in US-008, viewable by peers in
US-023, and removable by the creator in US-022. US-009 is a "should" — its absence does not
block the epic's acceptance.
